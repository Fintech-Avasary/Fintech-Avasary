## Decided
- 2 rounds in the demo, with the bank-or-buy choice between them. With 1 round the upgrade never takes effect and spending looks better than saving.
- Jobs are 2D games. Grocery Dash comes first because it's simpler to build than Pipe Fixer.
- Grocery Dash: 10 seconds to memorize the list, then a longer time to run the aisles.
- Emergencies are up to the Mayor: off, sometimes (50% chance) or always, so they don't happen every game.
- Emergencies charge wallet first, then bank, and the student can choose bank first instead. They can't refuse the charge.
