## Decided
- **Ways saving matters:** interest, a wallet-only shop, a lost-wallet event, and a fee to take savings out for bills. This overrides "interest-free" in §7.2, so `AGENTS.md` is updated.
- **Interest timing:** a Workday is one cycle of 2 rounds, and interest comes every 3 Workdays. It's rounded down to whole dollars.
- **Payday order:** purchases come out of the wallet first, then the save amount or percent applies to what's left.
- **Upgrade:** Food & Shelter can also be bought at the Round 2 payday, and it carries over to later Workdays.
