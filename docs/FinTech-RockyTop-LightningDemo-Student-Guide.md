How your demos work

Your playbook

Your team demos twice, 10 minutes each time, to two
different pairs of judges.

Each team has its own station. You stay put, and the judges
come to you.

You get set time slots for both demos ahead of the event.

The floor is yours. No
questions.

Judges jump in, ask questions, try
things

Between your demos

When you are not presenting to judges, expect visitors at your
station. All of our mentors, finance faculty and some university
administrators are invited, and they will be moving around the
room. They are not scoring you. Treat these conversations as
practice demos and a chance to get comfortable talking
about your product and your ideas.

These demos are about the product. A revenue model or
financial forecast is secondary at this point. If you have
something compelling there, feel free to include it.

What to bring

Judges may ask what is under the hood. These are examples
of what could come up, not a checklist you have to cover:

Required: your laptop and its power cord. You run your
demo from your own laptop.

The database it runs on, if it uses one

Optional: an external monitor you can plug in.

How the app gets its data, and whether users input data

Optional: printouts.

Where it is hosted and what it would cost to run

Which AI tools you used to build it

How you handle user data: what gets stored and who can
see it

Whether any data sources have terms of use or limits

What is live data and what is stand-in data

Optional: your phone, if your app works on one. Judges are
welcome to try it on your phone. We advise against asking
them to install it on their own phones, since that takes time
away from your demo.
